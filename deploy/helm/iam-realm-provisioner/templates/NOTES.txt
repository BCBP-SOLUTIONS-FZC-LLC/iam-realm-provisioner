iam-realm-provisioner {{ .Chart.Version }} deployed.

This service is the single writer to the Keycloak Admin API and the system
of record for tenant identity lifecycle (RP-1..RP-16), the three inbound
SQS consumers (membership-realm-q, tenant-realm-q, billing-realm-q), and
the iam.tenant.events topic's 9 published events.
